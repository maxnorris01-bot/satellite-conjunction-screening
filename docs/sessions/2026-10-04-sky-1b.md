# Session 2026-10-04: Sky view Phase 1b, header toggle and selection in Sky (portfolio-site)

Implements the 2026-10-04 "Sky view: toggle moves to the section header; selection in Sky
(Phase 1b)" working-notes entry, plus a phone layout fix found while verifying it.

- **Code:** in `portfolio-site`, on branch `feat/satellite-sky-1b`. Two commits, local only and
  not pushed:
  - `bc003fe` feat: move the Globe | Sky toggle to the section header and add selection in Sky
  - `c87f7e4` feat: keep the selected object visible in Sky on phones
- **Scope:** the sky geometry and the globe's own behaviour (selection, live neighbour, dimming,
  conjunction replay) are unchanged.

## What changed

### 1. The toggle is in the section header

- **State design: the page owns the mode and passes it down.** `SatelliteTool.tsx` holds
  `viewMode` and renders the toggle in the "Every tracked object" heading row. `SatelliteGlobe`
  takes `viewMode` as a prop (default `'globe'`) and no longer has its own copy or its own toggle.
  This was the simplest of the options in the entry. The page already owns the conjunction-replay
  `focus`, so two page-level rules follow naturally:
  - switching to Sky clears a running replay;
  - "Show on globe" sets the mode back to Globe before starting the replay.
- **Placement:** the toggle sits right-aligned on the heading's line at desktop widths. On a phone
  the row wraps, so it sits under the heading, still right-aligned (390 px: the heading alone fills
  the line).
- **Same control:** the same group labelled "View" with two `aria-pressed` buttons as before. It
  works with the keyboard (checked: Tab to it, Enter on "Sky" switches) and is visible in both
  modes. The legend panel no longer has it.

### 2. Selecting satellites in Sky

- **Picking** reuses the globe's `pickNearest` with the sky camera's view-projection:
  - 6 px pick radius with a mouse, 16 px with touch;
  - only objects above the horizon that aren't filtered out can be picked;
  - the same click-vs-drag threshold (5 px) as the globe, so a drag to look around never selects;
  - a gesture that ever had two pointers (a pinch) never selects.

  Selection is single, as on the globe: a new pick replaces the old one, and a click on empty
  sky, the close button or Deselect clears it. A click never changes the view direction or field
  of view.
- **The selected object** gets a ring and its name in a small label beside it. Dimming is
  recomputed for each view:
  - in Sky only the selected object stays at full strength;
  - on the globe it's the selected object plus its live nearest neighbour, as before.
- **Panel:** in Sky the panel adds azimuth, elevation and range (km from the observer) next to the
  existing altitude. They update live from an exact propagation (not the frame-sliced positions).
  The nearest-neighbour row and its note are globe-only.
- **Below the horizon:** a selected object that sets stays selected. Its ring and label hide, and
  the panel says "Below your horizon right now."
- **Across the toggle:** the selection now survives switching Globe and Sky. It's the same object
  in both, with each view's ring and dimming.
- **Pair and station views end on entering Sky.** Both are globe camera modes:
  - The page clears a conjunction replay.
  - The globe ends a station view and calls `clearFocusKeepInspect()`. That clears the pair rings,
    the miss line and the station group, and re-dims, but keeps an inspected object.
  - Neither path flies the globe camera home. The camera is released to free roam where it is, so
    returning to Globe never jumps.

### 3. The rest of Phase 1b

- **Reset view** (in the Your sky panel, folded and expanded): field of view back to 70°, facing
  the equator (south from the northern hemisphere, north from the southern), pitch 31° (horizon
  just above the bottom edge). It jumps rather than animating, so there's no motion to reduce.
- **Touch:** tap selects, drag looks around, pinch changes the field of view, and the end of a
  drag or pinch never selects.
- **Messages:** with no location the existing prompt shows. With a location the count line reads
  "N above your horizon", "Nothing above your horizon with the current filters." when filters hide
  everything, or "Nothing above your horizon right now." The legend keeps catalog counts.
- **Hint:** the Sky hint ends "… · click a point for details".

### 4. Phones: keep the selected object visible (second commit)

The first round's phone screenshot showed the stacked panels (legend at the top; Your sky plus the
selected object at the bottom) covering most of the sky, including the selected ring.

- **Two-line panel by default.** In Sky on a phone, the selected-object panel folds to its name and
  "Az … · El …" (or "Below your horizon right now"), with **Details** and the close button on the
  right.
  - Details (`aria-expanded`, controlling the details block) opens the full fields and Deselect;
    "Less" folds it again.
  - Each new selection starts folded.
  - Desktop always shows the full panel and never shows the Details button.
- **The side column moves out of the ring's way.** On a phone the side column docks along the
  bottom. When the ring would be under it, the column moves up to sit just below the legend.
  - It moves only if that actually uncovers the ring; otherwise it stays at the bottom.
  - A 16 px hysteresis stops it flickering at the boundary.
  - The rule is a pure function, `phoneSideDock` in `src/globe/layout.ts`, with 4 tests.
  - The component's 4 Hz tick feeds it the ring's screen position (a new engine read-out,
    `inspectedSkyScreen()`) and the measured stage, legend and column heights.
- **Your sky stays folded** on phones once a location is set, as before.
- **Height caps.** On a phone the column is capped at 64% of the stage, so an expanded panel
  scrolls instead of sliding over the legend. On desktop it's capped at the stage height and
  scrolls (thin scrollbar) instead of clipping its last lines.
- **Desktop tidy-ups:**
  - The Sky hint moved to the lower left, where the taller Sky side column used to cut it off.
  - The panel's "At <time> UTC" line is dropped in Sky, where the values are live and the clock
    sits just below the stage.

## Known limits and notes

- **A ring under the legend isn't moved out of the way.** On a phone the legend covers roughly the
  top 28% of the stage. A selection can end up there by a drag, by time passing, or carried over
  from the globe. The dock only handles the bottom stack (at 25% height the ring's edge touched
  the legend by 3 px). Dragging the view down uncovers it. If it matters, a foldable legend in Sky
  on phones would fix it.
- **The panel is a polite live region, as on the globe.** With live azimuth/elevation in it, a
  screen reader may announce updates often. Unchanged from the globe's existing panel, and worth a
  look in an accessibility pass.
- **The owner name has a missing space** ("European Organization for theExploitation of
  Meteorological Satellites (EUMETSAT)"). It's in the catalog's owner name, not the page.
